# Third-party software

This project loads Three.js version 0.187.0 from jsDelivr through the import map in `index.html`.

Three.js is copyright its authors and contributors and is distributed under the MIT License:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE

No third-party images, stock flower models, or external music tracks are used. The flower and butterfly artwork is generated procedurally in the browser, and the ambient soundscape is synthesized by the Web Audio API.
